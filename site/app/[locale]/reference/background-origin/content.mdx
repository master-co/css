## Examples \{#examples\}

### Choose the background positioning area \{#choose-the-background-positioning-area\}
Compare the same 80 × 50 image at the top-left of three positioning areas. The 8px border and 24px padding make each origin visible.

<DemoExample page="background-origin" section="choose-the-background-positioning-area" />

```html
<!-- Border origin · x 0, y 0 -->
<div id="target" role="img" aria-label="Geometric landscape"
  class="height:176px width:280px max-width:100% p-lg border-width:8px border-style:dashed border-color:color-mix(var(--color-violet-50),|transparent)
         background-clip:border-box background-repeat:no-repeat background-origin:border-box
         background-position:0|0 background-size:80px|50px
         background-image:url(/demo/landscape.svg)"
></div>
```

```html
<!-- Padding origin · x 8, y 8 -->
<div id="target" role="img" aria-label="Geometric landscape"
  class="height:176px width:280px max-width:100% p-lg border-width:8px border-style:dashed border-color:color-mix(var(--color-violet-50),|transparent)
         background-clip:border-box background-repeat:no-repeat background-origin:padding-box
         background-position:0|0 background-size:80px|50px
         background-image:url(/demo/landscape.svg)"
></div>
```

```html
<!-- Content origin · x 32, y 32 -->
<div id="target" role="img" aria-label="Geometric landscape"
  class="height:176px width:280px max-width:100% p-lg border-width:8px border-style:dashed border-color:color-mix(var(--color-violet-50),|transparent)
         background-clip:border-box background-repeat:no-repeat background-origin:content-box
         background-position:0|0 background-size:80px|50px
         background-image:url(/demo/landscape.svg)"
></div>
```

Offsets above are relative to the outer border edge. The dashed border may paint over the image. The clipping area stays `border-box` in all three specimens.

### Align art with borders or content \{#align-art-with-borders-or-content\}
Percentage image sizes also use the chosen positioning area. With `background-size:100%|100%`, the image fills either the border box or the content box; it may be distorted because both dimensions are forced.

<DemoExample page="background-origin" section="align-art-with-borders-or-content" />

```html
<!-- Fill the border positioning area -->
<div id="target" role="img" aria-label="Geometric landscape"
  class="height:176px width:280px max-width:100% p-lg border-width:8px border-style:dashed border-color:color-mix(var(--color-violet-50),|transparent)
         background-clip:border-box background-repeat:no-repeat background-origin:border-box
         background-position:0|0 background-size:100%|100%
         background-image:url(/demo/landscape.svg)"
></div>
```

```html
<!-- Fill the content positioning area -->
<div id="target" role="img" aria-label="Geometric landscape"
  class="height:176px width:280px max-width:100% p-lg border-width:8px border-style:dashed border-color:color-mix(var(--color-violet-50),|transparent)
         background-clip:border-box background-repeat:no-repeat background-origin:content-box
         background-position:0|0 background-size:100%|100%
         background-image:url(/demo/landscape.svg)"
></div>
```

Origin changes positioning and sizing, not clipping. Keep [background-clip](/reference/background-clip) separate. For `background-attachment:fixed`, the viewport supplies the positioning area instead.

### Apply conditionally \{#apply-conditionally\}
Widen the preview to `sm` to move the same image from the padding origin to the content origin. Print returns to padding-box positioning.

<DemoExample page="background-origin" section="apply-conditionally" />

```html
<!-- Padding → content at sm -->
<div id="target" role="img" aria-label="Geometric landscape"
  class="height:176px width:280px max-width:100% p-lg border-width:8px border-style:dashed border-color:color-mix(var(--color-violet-50),|transparent)
         background-clip:border-box background-repeat:no-repeat background-origin:padding-box
         background-position:0|0 background-size:80px|50px
         background-image:url(/demo/landscape.svg) background-origin:padding-box@media(print)
         background-origin:content-box@media(screen)@sm"
></div>
```

The border, padding, image size and clipping remain unchanged, making the origin shift visible.
