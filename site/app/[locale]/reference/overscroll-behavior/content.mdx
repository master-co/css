## Examples \{#examples\}
### Contain nested scrolling \{#contain-nested-scrolling\}
`overscroll-behavior:contain` keeps a gesture from chaining to an ancestor when the inner scroll area reaches its boundary. Send the inner collection to its end, then scroll over it again. Compare the outer position in the two panels.

<DemoExample page="overscroll-behavior" section="contain-nested-scrolling" />

```html
<div id="outer-scroll" class="overflow-y:auto h:14rem p-sm overscroll-behavior:contain"
  tabindex="0" aria-label="Outer collection">
  <!-- @MARK overscroll-behavior:contain -->
  <div id="inner-scroll" class="overflow-y:auto h:9rem overscroll-behavior:contain"
    tabindex="0" aria-label="Inner collection">
    <div class="h-xs">Inner collection</div>
  </div>
  <div class="h-xs mt-md">Outer collection continues</div>
</div>
```

### Suppress boundary effects on one axis \{#block-page-bounce-on-one-axis\}
`overscroll-behavior-y:none` blocks vertical chaining and the browser’s local vertical overscroll effect. `contain` still allows local effects. Rubber-band bounce, glow and navigation gestures depend on the browser and device, so compare their behavior on the platform you support.

<DemoExample page="overscroll-behavior" section="block-page-bounce-on-one-axis" />

```html
<div id="outer-scroll" class="overflow-y:auto h:14rem p-sm overscroll-behavior:contain"
  tabindex="0" aria-label="Outer collection">
  <!-- @MARK overscroll-behavior-y:none -->
  <div id="inner-scroll" class="overflow-y:auto h:9rem overscroll-behavior-y:none"
    tabindex="0" aria-label="Inner collection">
    <div class="h-xs">Inner collection</div>
  </div>
  <div class="h-xs mt-md">Outer collection continues</div>
</div>
```

### Keep horizontal gestures local \{#keep-horizontal-gestures-local\}
Use `overscroll-behavior-x:contain` on the inner horizontal scroller. At its right edge, another horizontal gesture stays inside that component instead of advancing the outer track.

<DemoExample page="overscroll-behavior" section="keep-horizontal-gestures-local" />

```html
<div id="outer-scroll" class="overflow-x:auto p-sm overscroll-behavior:contain">
  <div class="flex gap-md w:40rem">
    <!-- @MARK overscroll-behavior-x:contain -->
    <div id="inner-scroll" class="overflow-x:auto flex-shrink:0 h:9rem w:14rem overscroll-behavior-x:contain"
      tabindex="0" aria-label="Inner horizontal collection">
      <div class="h:100% w-md">Inner collection</div>
    </div>
    <div class="h:9rem w-xs">Outer collection continues</div>
  </div>
</div>
```

### Apply conditionally \{#apply-conditionally\}
Use [selectors](/reference/rules/selectors) and [conditional queries](/reference/rules/conditions) to apply the change only when its condition matches. The inner scroller contains chaining at narrow widths and allows it from `sm`. Resize, send the inner collection to its end, then try another gesture.

<DemoExample page="overscroll-behavior" section="apply-conditionally" />

```html
<div id="outer-scroll" class="overflow-y:auto h:14rem p-sm overscroll-behavior:contain"
  tabindex="0" aria-label="Outer collection">
  <!-- @MARK overscroll-behavior:contain -->
  <div id="inner-scroll" class="overflow-y:auto h:9rem overscroll-behavior:contain overscroll-behavior:auto@sm"
    tabindex="0" aria-label="Inner collection">
    <div class="h-xs">Inner collection</div>
  </div>
  <div class="h-xs mt-md">Outer collection continues</div>
</div>
```
