## Examples \{#examples\}
### Align tracks on both axes \{#align-grid-or-wrapped-flex-content-on-both-axes\}
`place-content:center` sets both `align-content:center` and `justify-content:center`. These fixed 80px columns and 48px rows form a compact grid inside a larger 280 × 224px container, leaving room to center the track group in both directions.

<DemoExample page="place-content" section="align-grid-or-wrapped-flex-content-on-both-axes" />

```html
<div id="layout" class="grid grid-template-columns:5rem|5rem grid-template-rows:3rem|3rem place-content:center gap-md h:14rem w:17.5rem max-w:100%">
  <div id="target" class="p-sm">01</div>
  <div id="peer" class="p-sm">02</div>
  <div id="item-3" class="p-sm">03</div>
  <div id="item-4" class="p-sm">04</div>
</div>
```

<details>
  <summary>Generated CSS</summary>
  <Class2CSS>
    {['grid', 'place-content:center']}
  </Class2CSS>
</details>

In Grid, the two components act on the block and inline axes. In a wrapping flex container they act on the cross-axis lines and main-axis items within each line. `align-content` has no effect on a `flex-nowrap` container.

### Use separate axis values \{#use-separate-axis-values\}
The first value sets `align-content`; the second sets `justify-content`. Here `start|space-between` keeps the rows at block-start while spreading the two fixed columns to the inline edges. With horizontal writing, this means top-aligned rows and columns at the left and right.

<DemoExample page="place-content" section="use-separate-axis-values" />

```html
<div id="layout" class="grid grid-template-columns:5rem|5rem grid-template-rows:3rem|3rem place-content:start|space-between gap-md h:14rem w:17.5rem max-w:100%">
  <div id="target" class="p-sm">01</div>
  <div id="peer" class="p-sm">02</div>
  <div id="item-3" class="p-sm">03</div>
  <div id="item-4" class="p-sm">04</div>
</div>
```

Use [place-items](/reference/place-items) for alignment inside each grid area. Fractional or stretched tracks can consume the space that would otherwise be available for content distribution.

### Apply conditionally \{#apply-conditionally\}
Use [conditional queries](/reference/rules/conditions) to change alignment at a real viewport width. The fixed track group starts at the top-left, then centers on both axes from `sm`. The container and track sizes stay fixed while the viewport condition changes.

<DemoExample page="place-content" section="apply-conditionally" />

```html
<div id="layout" class="grid grid-template-columns:5rem|5rem grid-template-rows:3rem|3rem place-content:start gap-md h:14rem w:17.5rem max-w:100% place-content:center@sm">
  <div id="target" class="p-sm">01</div>
  <div id="peer" class="p-sm">02</div>
  <div id="item-3" class="p-sm">03</div>
  <div id="item-4" class="p-sm">04</div>
</div>
```
