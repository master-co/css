## Examples \{#examples\}

### Position a background image \{#position-a-background-image\}
Use position shortcuts to align an image within its positioning area. This 160 × 100 image is smaller than its 320 × 160 frame, leaving visible free space.

<DemoExample page="background-position" section="position-a-background-image" />

```html
<!-- Left · vertical center -->
<div id="target" role="img" aria-label="Geometric landscape"
  class="height:160px w-xs max-width:100% background-position:left background-repeat:no-repeat
         background-size:160px|100px bg-surface-inset
         background-image:url(/demo/landscape.svg)"
></div>
```

```html
<!-- Center · both axes -->
<div id="target" role="img" aria-label="Geometric landscape"
  class="height:160px w-xs max-width:100% background-position:center background-repeat:no-repeat
         background-size:160px|100px bg-surface-inset
         background-image:url(/demo/landscape.svg)"
></div>
```

```html
<!-- Right · vertical center -->
<div id="target" role="img" aria-label="Geometric landscape"
  class="height:160px w-xs max-width:100% background-repeat:no-repeat background-position:right
         background-size:160px|100px bg-surface-inset
         background-image:url(/demo/landscape.svg)"
></div>
```

<details>
  <summary>Generated CSS</summary>
  <Class2CSS>
    {['background-position:left', 'background-position:center', 'background-position:right']}
  </Class2CSS>
</details>

A single horizontal keyword keeps the vertical axis centered. `bg-top` and `bg-bottom` center the horizontal axis.

### Use explicit positions \{#use-explicit-positions\}
A two-value position supplies horizontal then vertical offsets. Percentages align matching points on the image and positioning area; lengths offset the image from the top-left.

<DemoExample page="background-position" section="use-explicit-positions" />

```html
<!-- 50% 25% · shares the free space -->
<div id="target" role="img" aria-label="Geometric landscape"
  class="height:160px w-xs max-width:100% background-repeat:no-repeat background-position:50%|25%
         background-size:160px|100px bg-surface-inset
         background-image:url(/demo/landscape.svg)"
></div>
```

```html
<!-- 24px 12px · measured offset -->
<div id="target" role="img" aria-label="Geometric landscape"
  class="height:160px w-xs max-width:100% background-repeat:no-repeat background-position:var(--spacing-lg)|var(--spacing-sm)
         background-size:160px|100px bg-surface-inset
         background-image:url(/demo/landscape.svg)"
></div>
```

At the full 320 × 160 frame size, `50% 25%` gives x = (320 − 160) × 50% = 80px and y = (160 − 100) × 25% = 15px. On narrow screens the frame shrinks, so the horizontal result changes. If the image fills an axis exactly, percentages on that axis produce no movement.

### Apply conditionally \{#apply-conditionally\}
The image covers a deliberately shallow frame. Widen to `sm` to move its crop from center to top; print restores the center crop.

<DemoExample page="background-position" section="apply-conditionally" />

```html
<!-- Centered crop → top crop at sm -->
<div id="target" role="img" aria-label="Geometric landscape"
  class="height:120px w-xs max-width:100% background-position:center background-size:cover background-repeat:no-repeat
         background-image:url(/demo/landscape.svg) background-position:center@media(print) background-position:top@media(screen)@sm"
></div>
```

Cover crops vertically here because the frame is wider than the source’s 8:5 ratio. Position moves the cropped image without changing its rendered size.
