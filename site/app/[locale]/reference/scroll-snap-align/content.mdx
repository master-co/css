## Examples \{#examples\}
### Align a snap item to the start \{#align-a-snap-item-to-the-start\}
`snap-start` makes an item’s leading edge a snap position inside a snapping container. The complete track below has more content than fits in its viewport. Scroll it or choose Panel 4 to see start alignment.

<DemoExample page="scroll-snap-align" section="align-a-snap-item-to-the-start" />

```html
<div id="scrollport" class="flex overflow-x:auto gap:md scroll-snap-type:x|mandatory"
  tabindex="0" aria-label="Horizontal panels">
  <article id="panel-1" class="flex-shrink:0 h:36x w:48x snap-start">Panel 1</article>
  <article id="panel-2" class="flex-shrink:0 h:36x w:48x snap-start">Panel 2</article>
  <article id="panel-3" class="flex-shrink:0 h:36x w:48x snap-start">Panel 3</article>
  <article id="panel-4" class="flex-shrink:0 h:36x w:48x snap-start">Panel 4</article>
  <article id="panel-5" class="flex-shrink:0 h:36x w:48x snap-start">Panel 5</article>
  <article id="panel-6" class="flex-shrink:0 h:36x w:48x snap-start">Panel 6</article>
</div>
```

<details>
  <summary>Generated CSS</summary>

  </Class2CSS>

### Center snap items \{#center-snap-items\}
`snap-center` aligns the item’s center with the snapport’s center. Choose an interior item such as Panel 4: the first and last items can be constrained by the track’s scroll range.

<DemoExample page="scroll-snap-align" section="center-snap-items" />

```html
<div id="scrollport" class="flex overflow-x:auto gap:md scroll-snap-type:x|mandatory"
  tabindex="0" aria-label="Horizontal panels">
  <article id="panel-1" class="flex-shrink:0 h:36x w:48x snap-center">Panel 1</article>
  <article id="panel-2" class="flex-shrink:0 h:36x w:48x snap-center">Panel 2</article>
  <article id="panel-3" class="flex-shrink:0 h:36x w:48x snap-center">Panel 3</article>

  <article id="panel-6" class="flex-shrink:0 h:36x w:48x snap-center">Panel 6</article>
</div>
```

### Disable item snapping \{#disable-item-snapping\}
`scroll-snap-align:none` removes an item’s snap position. Panel 2 still occupies space in the track; The other panels remain snap candidates. Scroll across the second panel to compare the stops.

<DemoExample page="scroll-snap-align" section="disable-item-snapping" />

```html
<div id="scrollport" class="flex overflow-x:auto gap:md scroll-snap-type:x|mandatory"
  tabindex="0" aria-label="Horizontal panels">
  <article id="panel-1" class="flex-shrink:0 h:36x w:48x snap-start">Panel 1</article>
  <article id="panel-2" class="flex-shrink:0 h:36x w:48x scroll-snap-align:none">Panel 2</article>
  <article id="panel-3" class="flex-shrink:0 h:36x w:48x snap-start">Panel 3</article>
  <article id="panel-4" class="flex-shrink:0 h:36x w:48x snap-start">Panel 4</article>
  <article id="panel-5" class="flex-shrink:0 h:36x w:48x snap-start">Panel 5</article>
  <article id="panel-6" class="flex-shrink:0 h:36x w:48x snap-start">Panel 6</article>
</div>
```

### Apply conditionally \{#apply-conditionally\}
Use [selectors](/reference/rules/selectors) and [conditional queries](/reference/rules/conditions) to apply the change only when its condition matches. The cards snap to the start by default and to the center from `sm`. Widen the preview and choose Panel 4 to compare the alignment.

<DemoExample page="scroll-snap-align" section="apply-conditionally" />

```html
<div id="scrollport" class="flex overflow-x:auto gap:md scroll-snap-type:x|mandatory"
  tabindex="0" aria-label="Horizontal panels">
  <article id="panel-1" class="flex-shrink:0 h:36x w:48x snap-start snap-center@sm">Panel 1</article>
  <article id="panel-2" class="flex-shrink:0 h:36x w:48x snap-start snap-center@sm">Panel 2</article>
  <article id="panel-3" class="flex-shrink:0 h:36x w:48x snap-start snap-center@sm">Panel 3</article>
  <article id="panel-4" class="flex-shrink:0 h:36x w:48x snap-start snap-center@sm">Panel 4</article>
  <article id="panel-5" class="flex-shrink:0 h:36x w:48x snap-start snap-center@sm">Panel 5</article>
  <article id="panel-6" class="flex-shrink:0 h:36x w:48x snap-start snap-center@sm">Panel 6</article>
</div>
```
