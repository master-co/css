### Use logical alignment
Use `text:start` and `text:end` when alignment should follow the writing direction instead of always mapping to physical left or right.
```html
<!-- @MARK text:start --><!-- @MARK text:end -->
<p class="text:start">Start-aligned content</p>
<p class="text:end">End-aligned content</p>
```

Use physical `text:left` and `text:right` when the design explicitly requires those sides regardless of direction.

### Apply conditionally
Apply styles based on different states using [selectors](/guide/state-selectors) and [conditional queries](/guide/conditional-queries).
```html
<div class="text:start text:center@sm text:end@lg text:start@print">...</div>
```
