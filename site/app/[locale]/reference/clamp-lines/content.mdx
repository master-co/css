## Usage

The `clamp-lines(n)` mixin sets the box layout, line limit and overflow treatment together. Supply a positive integer. Keep the full content available through an accessible reading path.

### Limit a preview to two lines \{#limit-a-preview-to-two-lines\}

<DemoExample page="clamp-lines" section="limit-a-preview-to-two-lines" />

```html
<p id="target" class="width:18rem max-width:100% clamp-lines(2)">A shared design language helps a team make consistent decisions. This preview keeps two lines visible while the complete description remains available below.</p>
<details><summary>Read the full description</summary><p>A shared design language helps a team make consistent decisions. This preview keeps two lines visible while the complete description remains available below.</p></details>
```
