## Examples \{#examples\}
### Align grid items on both axes \{#align-grid-items-on-both-axes\}
`place-items:center` sets both `align-items:center` and `justify-items:center`. Each label centers within its own column and 160px row. The grid tracks keep their full size; only the items move inside their areas.

<DemoExample page="place-items" section="align-grid-items-on-both-axes" />

```html
<div id="layout" class="grid grid-cols:2 grid-template-rows:160px place-items:center gap:md w:70x max-w:full">
  <div id="target" class="p:sm">Alpha</div>
  <div id="peer" class="p:sm">Beta</div>
</div>
```

<details>
  <summary>Generated CSS</summary>
  <Class2CSS>
    {['grid', 'place-items:center']}
  </Class2CSS>
</details>

For different values, put block-axis alignment first and inline-axis alignment second, such as `place-items:start|center`.

### Stretch automatic-sized items \{#stretch-items\}
`place-items:stretch` fills both dimensions of a grid area when the corresponding item sizes are automatic. The blue item has no width or height, so it fills its area. The violet item keeps its explicitly set 80 × 64px size.

<DemoExample page="place-items" section="stretch-items" />

```html
<div id="layout" class="grid grid-cols:2 grid-template-rows:160px place-items:stretch gap:md w:70x max-w:full">
  <div id="target" class="p:sm">Auto size</div>
  <div id="peer" class="h:16x w:20x p:sm">80 × 64</div>
</div>
```

Minimum and maximum sizes still apply. Use [place-self](/reference/place-self) when just one child needs an override.

### Apply conditionally \{#apply-conditionally\}
Use [conditional queries](/reference/rules/conditions) to change alignment at a real viewport width. Both automatic-sized labels fill their areas initially, then take intrinsic sizes and center in those same areas from `sm`.

<DemoExample page="place-items" section="apply-conditionally" />

```html
<div id="layout" class="grid grid-cols:2 grid-template-rows:160px place-items:stretch gap:md w:70x max-w:full place-items:center@sm">
  <div id="target" class="p:sm">Alpha</div>
  <div id="peer" class="p:sm">Beta</div>
</div>
```

For flex items, only the `align-items` half affects alignment. `justify-items` does not distribute a flex line’s free space; use [justify-content](/reference/justify-content) for that.
