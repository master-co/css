## Guide status [sr-only]
This Sass migration guide is being rebuilt. Use the [quick start](/guide/migration) to direct your local coding agent, then ask it to inspect Sass-specific styling before changing code.

## Inspect first
- `.scss` and `.sass` entry files, partials, imports, `@use`, and `@forward`.
- Variables, maps, mixins, functions, placeholders, and generated selectors.
- Component styles that can become markup classes or `@components` definitions.
- Vendor imports, generated CSS, and complex nesting that should stay in regular CSS.
