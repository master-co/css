## Overview [sr-only]
<Default />

In Master CSS, most utilities are defined and implemented through `config.utilities`. You can create your own custom utilities and use them directly within the `class=""` attribute.

These custom utilities are fully compatible with Master CSS’s advanced features, including selectors, at-rules, functions, and variables—offering a powerful and flexible styling experience.

Use this as a last resort when implementing your design system or styling conventions. Avoid creating custom utilities unless absolutely necessary, as overuse can lead to reduced maintainability and consistency across your codebase.

---

## Add a custom utility
Create your own utility.
<Overview />
Master CSS does not provide methods like `declare` or `transform` to let you fully customize the syntax, but instead exposes limited `transformers` and `declarers` to let you extend practical utilities. You should rely on the implicit behavior built into Master CSS to achieve the desired effect, but more often than not you will need `config.components`.
