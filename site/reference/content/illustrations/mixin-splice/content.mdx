# Reference prose: illustrations/mixin-splice

## title \{#copy-title\}

A mixin expands at the call position

## alt \{#copy-alt\}

A highlighted apply line in a native caption rule is replaced by two highlighted declarations, between the unchanged color and font-weight lines.

## caption \{#copy-caption\}

Matching lines stay aligned around the insertion. The selector, declaration order and surrounding layer remain the caller’s.

## description-0 \{#copy-description-0\}

The --caption mixin supplies font-size: 14px and line-height: 1.5.

## description-1 \{#copy-description-1\}

Inside .caption, @apply --caption expands between color: currentColor and font-weight: 600.

## description-2 \{#copy-description-2\}

Expansion does not register a class or move the native rule to a generated layer. Duplicate declarations and authored order are preserved.
