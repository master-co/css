# Reference prose: illustrations/where-specificity

## title \{#copy-title\}

where() and is() can match the same states with different weight

## alt \{#copy-alt\}

Two aligned selector decompositions keep the owner’s 0-1-0 contribution. where adds zero; is adds the most specific argument, 1-0-0.

## caption \{#copy-caption\}

The figures compare specificity only. Origin, importance and layer precedence are considered earlier in the cascade.

## description-0 \{#copy-description-0\}

The escaped complete class owner contributes 0-1-0 in both generated selectors.

## description-1 \{#copy-description-1\}

For :where(:hover,#selected), the suffix contributes 0-0-0, so the total is 0-1-0.

## description-2 \{#copy-description-2\}

For :is(:hover,#selected), the ID argument supplies the maximum argument specificity of 1-0-0, giving a total of 1-1-0 even when the hover alternative is the one matching.
