# Reference prose: illustrations/layer-token-separation

## title \{#copy-title\}

Selecting a style layer does not move its token

## alt \{#copy-alt\}

p-md@layer(defaults) maps to a padding rule in defaults, while its spacing-md dependency remains in theme.

## caption \{#copy-caption\}

The two layer bands show output ownership. Their positions are not a complete cascade comparison.

## description-0 \{#copy-description-0\}

p-md@layer(defaults) emits the generated padding declaration in the defaults layer.

## description-1 \{#copy-description-1\}

Its --spacing-md dependency still emits under :root,:host in the theme layer. @layer(defaults) selects the class rule’s layer, not its dependency’s layer.
