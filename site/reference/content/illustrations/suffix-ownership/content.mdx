## caption \{#copy-caption\}

## description-0 \{#copy-description-0\}

p(12) supplies padding. :hover tests the class-bearing element, @sm limits the rule to the named media condition, and the final ! marks its declaration important.

## description-1 \{#copy-description-1\}

The separate r-md class has none of those suffixes. It can apply without hover or the sm condition and its declaration is not important.
