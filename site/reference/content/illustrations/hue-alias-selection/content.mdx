# Reference prose: illustrations/hue-alias-selection

## title \{#copy-title\}

Hue aliases do not share one step number

## alt \{#copy-alt\}

A blue alias connects to step 60 in a blue swatch strip; a yellow alias connects to step 40 in an otherwise identically arranged yellow strip.

## caption \{#copy-caption\}

The preset blue alias references blue-60; yellow references yellow-40. The outlines mark those references, not a border added by either background class. Selected palette steps are shown.

## description-0 \{#copy-description-0\}

bg-blue reads --color-blue, which references --color-blue-60 in the preset. The blue strip marks step 60.

## description-1 \{#copy-description-1\}

bg-yellow reads --color-yellow, which references --color-yellow-40. The yellow strip marks step 40 in the same set of displayed steps.

## description-2 \{#copy-description-2\}

Both strips show actual palette colors at steps 10, 20, 40, 60, 80 and 90. Equal swatch widths are for comparison, not a scale of perceptual distance.

## description-3 \{#copy-description-3\}

The two alias chains remain the same in light and dark schemes. Redefining an alias or its referenced token can change its result.
