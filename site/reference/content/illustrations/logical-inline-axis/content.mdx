# Reference prose: illustrations/logical-inline-axis

## title \{#copy-title\}

Inline padding follows the writing mode

## alt \{#copy-alt\}

px(16) creates left and right padding in horizontal writing, but top and bottom padding in vertical writing.

## caption \{#copy-caption\}

The two square content boxes have the same dimensions. Tinted bands identify the actual 16px padding-inline regions.

## description-0 \{#copy-description-0\}

px(16) emits padding-inline: calc(16 * 1px). In horizontal-tb, its two insets are on the left and right.

## description-1 \{#copy-description-1\}

In vertical-rl, the inline axis runs vertically, so the same class puts padding on the top and bottom.

## description-2 \{#copy-description-2\}

Master CSS x and y names refer to inline and block axes. Physical t, r, b and l names keep their physical sides.
