`DemoMedia` without `src` draws the site’s mountain artwork as an inline SVG on a sheet, painted with `currentColor` from `tone`. With `src` and `alt` it renders the site `Image` with its intrinsic `width` and `height`.

```tsx
<DemoMedia tone="blue" className="w-full h-auto" aria-label="Blue mountain illustration" />
<DemoMedia src="/demo/landscape.svg" alt="Sun above layered mountains" width={320} height={200} />
```

The component adds no layout; dimensions, radius and float belong to the example.
