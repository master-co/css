`DemoMeasure` wraps its children in `div.ui-measure`: a `ui-measure-head` with the label and an `output` of the measured width and height, a `ui-measure-rule`, and the measured box. A `ResizeObserver` keeps the reading current.

```tsx
<DemoMeasure label="Content bounds">
  <DemoSurface className="p-md">…</DemoSurface>
</DemoMeasure>
```

```html
<div class="ui-measure">
  <div class="ui-measure-head"><span>Content bounds</span><output>300 × 96 px</output></div>
  <div class="ui-measure-rule" aria-hidden="true"></div>
  <div>…</div>
</div>
```

Never insert the wrapper between a layout parent and its items; measure the parent or a dedicated wrapper instead.
