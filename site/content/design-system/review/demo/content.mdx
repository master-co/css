`Demo` renders one `figure.ui-tray`: an optional `ui-tray-head` (`title`, `description`, `controls`), a `ui-stage` holding the children, an optional `source`, and a `ui-tray-caption`. `className` and `style` belong to the stage, so the chrome never joins the demonstrated layout. The stage centers its specimen; `align="start"` places it from the start edge.

```tsx
<Demo title="Collection" caption="The card follows its container." source={<>
  <Code lang="html" name="HTML">{html}</Code>
  <DemoGeneratedCSS><Code lang="css" beautify>{css}</Code></DemoGeneratedCSS>
</>}>
  <DemoSurface className="p-lg">Room for the details.</DemoSurface>
</Demo>
```

```html
<figure class="ui-tray">
  <figcaption class="ui-tray-head"><div><p class="ui-tray-title">Collection</p></div></figcaption>
  <div class="ui-stage">…</div>
  <div class="ui-code">…</div>
  <details class="ui-disclosure"><summary>Generated CSS</summary>…</details>
  <div class="ui-tray-caption">The card follows its container.</div>
</figure>
```

`padding="sm"` and `padding="none"` map to `data-padding` on the stage for scenes with their own spacing. Add `frame="none"` when the content already owns its frame: a `DemoViewport` browser, a `ui-compare` or a code window. `DemoGeneratedCSS` is always closed by default. Keep labels and controls outside a measured flex, grid or float parent.

### Purposeful backgrounds \{#background-choices\}

Choose the stage background for the behavior being explained; most examples use the plain lit stage. `background` maps to `data-background` on `ui-stage`.
