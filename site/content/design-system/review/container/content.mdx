`DemoContainer` takes `title`, `children` and optional `maxWidth` (720 by default). Its native range changes the measured wrapper; Fit restores the available width.

```tsx
<DemoContainer title="Card width" maxWidth={720}>
  <div className="container-type:inline-size">
    <article className="p-md">The card owns its containment.</article>
  </div>
</DemoContainer>
```

The page viewport remains unchanged. Keep the query boundary and responsive descendants in the example.
