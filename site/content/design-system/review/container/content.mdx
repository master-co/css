`DemoContainer` renders a complete tray: `title` and the width tools in the `ui-tray-head`, the measured wrapper on a `ui-stage`, and an optional `caption`. `maxWidth` (720 by default) bounds the range; Fit restores the available width.

```tsx
<DemoContainer title="Card width" caption="The card stacks below 18rem." maxWidth={720}>
  <div className="container-inline">
    <article className="p-md">The card owns its containment.</article>
  </div>
</DemoContainer>
```

The page viewport remains unchanged. Keep the query boundary and responsive descendants in the example; the wrapper carries `data-demo-container` and only its width.
