`DemoBadge` renders `span.ui-badge` with `tone`, `variant` (`soft` or `dot`) and `size` (`sm` or `md`) mapped to data attributes. Badges are annotations: keep them outside measured flex, grid or float layouts.

```tsx
<DemoBadge tone="green" variant="dot">Supported</DemoBadge>
<DemoBadge size="sm">Static</DemoBadge>
```

```html
<span class="ui-badge" data-tone="green" data-variant="dot">Supported</span>
<span class="ui-badge" data-size="sm">Static</span>
```
