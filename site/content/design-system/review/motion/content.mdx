`DemoMotion` wraps animated children and adds a `div.ui-player` with Play/Pause, Replay and the current state. Animations start paused (`data-playback="paused"` holds them before hydration) and pause again when the reduced-motion preference changes.

```tsx
<DemoMotion>
  <DemoItem tone="blue" className="(animation-name:rotate) (animation-duration:2s) (animation-iteration-count:infinite)">↗</DemoItem>
</DemoMotion>
```

```html
<div class="ui-player" role="group" aria-label="Animation controls" data-state="paused">
  <button type="button" class="ui-button" data-size="sm">Play</button>
  <button type="button" class="ui-button" data-size="sm" data-variant="quiet">Replay</button>
  <span class="ui-player-time"><output>Paused</output></span>
</div>
```

Iframe documents use `DemoViewport motion` instead; the viewport’s own Play and Replay drive the document timeline.
