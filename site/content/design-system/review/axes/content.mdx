`DemoAxes` renders `div.ui-axes`: a `ui-axes-inline` arrow along the top, a `ui-axes-block` arrow down the start edge and the subject in `ui-axes-content`. `inline` and `block` replace the default “Main axis” and “Cross axis” names.

```tsx
<DemoAxes inline="Inline axis" block="Block axis">
  <DemoSurface className="flex gap-sm p-md">…</DemoSurface>
</DemoAxes>
```

```html
<div class="ui-axes">
  <div class="ui-axes-inline"><span>Main axis</span><span class="ui-axes-rule"></span></div>
  <div class="ui-axes-block"><span>Cross axis</span><span class="ui-axes-rule"></span></div>
  <div class="ui-axes-content">…</div>
</div>
```
