The [brand guide](/brand) defines the identity. This handbook is the interface it runs on: Moonstone in light mode and Obsidian in dark, one material ladder, one elevation order and one way to frame an example.

## Layers and materials \{#foundations\}

Every surface is a step on one ladder: the canvas holds a tray, a tray holds a lit stage, and sheets rise from it. A well sits below its surroundings. The interface stays monochrome; hue appears only in named teaching specimens and status.

<Materials />

## Controls that fit \{#interaction\}

Controls share one material and one state order. A raised face lifts half a pixel on hover and sinks into a well when pressed. Selected segments rise inside their sunken track, and focus is always a visible ink ring.

<Controls />

## A clear reading rhythm \{#documentation\}

One example is one tray: its head names it, the stage shows it, the code window holds the source with a closed Generated CSS disclosure, and a caption says what to notice. Tables and indexes own their frames.
