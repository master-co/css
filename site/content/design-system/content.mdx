## Layers and materials \{#foundations\}

A pale surround seats the content surface. One fine contour defines its shape; a short contact shadow adds separation when needed. Solid surfaces stay quiet, with text keeping its contrast and its place.

## Controls that fit \{#interaction\}

A selected surface sits inside its track. Native buttons, ranges and selects keep their familiar behavior, with clear focus and a steady position through each state.

## A clear reading rhythm \{#documentation\}

Keep names, values and supporting details aligned. Code and tables own their frames; longer explanations remain nearby in native disclosures.
