## Identity \{#identity\}

Master CSS is a markup-driven CSS orchestration language for developers and AI agents. Its identity makes the same promise as the product: decisions are explicit, relationships are readable, and the details stay connected.

- **Clarity in the foreground.** The work comes first; text, code and data each have a clear place.
- **A system you can read.** Shared values, a steady rhythm and deliberate states connect the parts.
- **Precision with warmth.** The structure stays neutral. Gold adds one small, recognizable point of warmth.

This page defines the mark, its gold, the typefaces and the voice. The [Design System](/design-system) carries them into the interface: materials, controls, layout and motion.

## Color \{#color-system\}

Gold identifies the brand; the interface around it stays neutral. Give each view one gold focus, such as the mark or a single signal beside the action that matters, and let words carry status and readable text.

<BrandGold />

Both versions of the mark share a continuous vertical champagne-to-gold gradient with soft wing shading; keep every stop in the supplied SVG. Neutral roles, materials and status colors belong to the [Design System](/design-system#foundations), and the complete palette to the [color reference](/reference/tokens/color).

## Typography \{#typography\}

Geist carries the interface and Latin text, Noto Sans TC carries Traditional Chinese, and IBM Plex Mono carries code, identifiers and aligned numbers. Fall back to the system sans-serif or monospace when a family is unavailable.

<BrandTypefaces />

Keep Chinese tracking natural, use tabular figures for values that change or align, and let a long heading wrap instead of shrinking it. The type scale is in the [text reference](/reference/tokens/text).

## Voice \{#voice\}

Write as a thoughtful collaborator. Start with the reader's task, name the thing precisely and explain the result in plain words. Keep claims close to what the product can demonstrate.

| Context | Clear and useful | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Introduce the product | Style in markup with class names and design tokens. | The ultimate, revolutionary styling experience. |
| Guide an action | Copy the color variable. | Unlock your palette's full potential. |
| Explain a result | Copied `var(--color-amber-40)`. | Success! Everything is ready to go. |
| Write in Traditional Chinese | 在標記中編寫樣式，使用共用的設計 tokens。 | 透過極致創新的方案，全面賦能設計體驗。 |

Use sentence case for headings and write “Master CSS” in prose. English and Traditional Chinese carry the same meaning; keep API names and class strings as they are. A message says what happened and, when needed, the useful next step.

### You may not

- Use “Master CSS” or the logo in the name of your product, domain or company, such as `mastercss-tools.io` or `Master CSS UI`.
- Present your project as official or endorsed unless we have formally partnered.
- Sell goods such as T-shirts or stickers, or place the logo on merchandise, without written consent.
- Modify the logo's color, shape or orientation, or add shadows, gradients or other visual effects.
- Use the logo as part of your own logo or product identity.

### Name usage \{#name-usage\}

Write the name as **Master CSS**, as in “Built with Master CSS” or “Powered by Master CSS”. Do not write it as `mastercss`, `MASTERcss` or `CSSMaster`.
