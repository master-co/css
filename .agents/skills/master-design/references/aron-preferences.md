# Aron preferences — opt-in

Use only when selected by the user or project instructions. Preserve brand and functional contrast. This is a preference, not a historical design movement or universal quality standard.

Clean near-white, quiet almost-neutral grays, almost-flat surfaces and short naturally fading shadows. Keep clear text and compact consistent navigation. Use restrained color for meaningful actions, states and data. Solid surfaces have no default inner white rim. Press feedback leaves content stable. Glass stays local with a faint dark contour and diffuse depth.

| Icon fade / panel retarget | 150ms ease-out / 200ms cubic-bezier(.2,0,0,1) |

Tune for actual size, base, role and theme. A faint card contour is not automatically an adequate checkbox/input boundary or focus cue. The 18% ring comparison illustrates compensation rather than a default.

Use project fonts. The article used Geist and IBM Plex Mono; neither is an installation requirement. Optical corrections, tracking and cap/baseline trimming remain glyph- and script-specific.

For diagrams: concise English, one main relationship, measured guides and at most two annotation groups. Distinguish geometric enlargement from native scale. The study's canvas is 1440×1000, exported at 2× as PNG/WebP.

For overlapping avatars, keep the image inner stroke and opaque host-colored separation ring independent. The separate study starts at D32/O8/G2, black 10% inward stroke in light and white 8% in dark; the existing dashboard retains D22/O5/G1.5. These are opt-in specimen values, not a global avatar sizing rule. See [Avatar stacking](avatar-stacks.md).
