- Static roots now hold a permanent reference across their dependency graph, including roots provided by external CSS. Existing emitted counts still suppress duplicate local declarations. Unregistration uses the corresponding iterative traversal. Inline intermediates are not emitted, but traversal continues through their dependency edges to resources referenced by substituted values.
